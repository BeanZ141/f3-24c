Frequently Asked Questions

Booking & Reservations

How do I book a flight ticket?

To book a flight ticket:

  1. Go to the Flights section
  2. Enter your departure and destination cities
  3. Select your travel dates and passenger count
  4. Click "Search" to view available flights
  5. Choose your preferred flight and click "Book"
  6. Complete the payment process
Can I cancel or modify my booking?

Yes, you can cancel or modify your booking depending on the fare rules:

  • Log in to your account and go to "My Bookings"
  • Select the booking you want to modify
  • Choose "Cancel" or "Modify" option
  • Cancellation charges may apply based on the fare type
How do I get my ticket after booking?

After successful payment, your digital ticket will be:

  • Sent to your registered email address
  • Available in your dashboard under "My Tickets"
  • Accessible via QR code for easy check-in

Payment & Refunds

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept the following payment methods:

  • Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express)
  • Debit Cards
  • Net Banking
  • UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm)
  • Digital Wallets
How long does it take to process refunds?

Refund processing times vary by payment method:

  • Credit/Debit Cards: 5-7 business days
  • Net Banking: 3-5 business days
  • UPI/Wallets: 1-3 business days

QR Code & Check-in

How do I use the QR code for check-in?

Using your QR code is simple:

  1. Open your digital ticket from email or dashboard
  2. Present the QR code at the venue/airport
  3. Staff will scan the code for verification
  4. Your attendance will be logged automatically
